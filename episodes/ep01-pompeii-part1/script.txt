This is Tony! Today, he travels to ancient Pompeii!


By tomorrow morning, this whole city will be buried.
Tony is a baker now. It's the year 79 AD. A normal day.
The oven is hot. The bread is almost ready.
For days, the ground has been shaking a little.
Just after midday, Vesuvius explodes.
A column of ash and smoke shoots miles into the sky.
It spreads out like a giant pine tree.
No one here saw this coming.
Soon after, small white stones start falling.
Pumice. Light. Not dangerous, at first.
They rattle on the roof tiles like hail.
The sky turns grey. The afternoon feels like evening.
His neighbors are already running for the city gates.
His wife is holding their daughter. They're waiting for a decision.

For now, inside the house, they're safe from the stones.
The walls are thick. The doors are strong.
But the stones keep coming.
They pile up on the roof. Hour after hour.
Outside, the stones are already knee-deep.
In the street, people tie pillows to their heads.
His daughter asks if the gods have left them.
The ground shakes again. The roof beam above them creaks.

Now you decide for Tony. Right now!
Option A: run for the city gates, through the falling stones.
Option B: stay inside and wait it out.
