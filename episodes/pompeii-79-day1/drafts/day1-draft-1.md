Tony is early and in the wrong place, which is the joke. Stones were light and up to about 3 cm (pompeii-009).
- **C (troll bait, narrator recommends): "Sell the stones."** Tomorrow: Tony opens a souvenir stand in the street. Real history pays it off: the pumice piles up about 15 cm per hour (pompeii-008), so the stand is buried quickly, and most of the town is leaving (pompeii-014). Nobody buys, no fact is changed.

## FICTION NOTE

Fiction (not history): everything Tony does or suffers (the cart, the shelf of jars, the house he hides in, the stones hitting him, the freeze frame stone), the NPCs hopping over the stepping stones and shrugging at the tremors as comedy staging, the SURVIVAL meter (88, 72, 51, 12 are a joke meter), the souvenir stand, the pillow helmet idea, and Tony's time travel. "Pompeii shrugs" is the narrator's opinion, backed by pompeii-023. The house and its roof are generic; no claim is made that it stood a particular way. The roof fact (pompeii-010) is stated as a general fact about the town, with a hedge ("most", "about"), and nobody under a collapsed roof is shown or mocked. The Pliny the Younger line describes his comparison of the cloud; he watched it from Misenum, the image over Pompeii is a visual stand-in. The pillow idea (option B) is documented only at Stabiae that night (pompeii-017); it is never presented as happening in Pompeii.
